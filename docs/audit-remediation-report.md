## F01 — Version compatibility

### Reproduction and source location
Reproduced on the unmodified tree (2026-09-19) against the frozen historical schemas:
- `packages/cdm/synapse_cdm/version.py::compatible()` returned `w_major == r_major`, so
  `compatible("2.1.0", "2.0.0")` was `True`, `compatible("2.9.0", "2.1.0")` was `True`, and
  `parse()` split on dots and called `int()`, so `compatible("2.1.0\n", "2.1.0")` and
  `compatible("-1.0.0", "-1.0.0")` were `True` (`int()` strips whitespace and reads a sign).
- The v2.0.0 schemas (`git show v2.0.0:schemas/*.schema.json`) carry `additionalProperties: false`
  and refuse a current document that carries `quality`, `status` or `residual` (and `area`,
  `route`, `validity` on plan_object) whether populated or explicitly `null`:
  `Additional properties are not allowed ('quality' was unexpected)`. Nested 2.1.0 additions
  (`source.transformations`, `position.vertical`) are refused the same way.
- The current-contract prose in `MIGRATIONS.md` ("a 1.0.0 reader accepts a 1.2.0 object"),
  `docs/docs/changelog.mdx`, `docs/docs/schema-reference/index.mdx` and `SECURITY.md` stated the
  unsupported direction as a guarantee. Two tests asserted it (`tests/test_cdm_models.py` lines
  115 and 606-610).
- Package tag and CDM schema version are separate axes: v2.1.0, v2.1.2 and v2.2.0 all ship CDM
  2.1.0 (the v2.2.0 copy differs from v2.1.0's by descriptions only — sha256s recorded).

### Risk
A consumer pinned to an older CDM minor that trusted `compatible()` would attempt ingest of newer
objects and fail at validation, or — with a permissive parser — silently drop unknown fields. The
helper also presumed unpublished future minors safe, so a wire version typo or a future release
passed the version gate with no evidence. Malformed versions (`"2.1.0\n"`, negatives, leading
zeroes) passed the same gate while the model's `schema_version` validator refused them, so the
two checks disagreed on what a version is.

### Correction, contract and version implications

### Risk
